> The critique you give is graded the same as the work you bring.

## What to do

Show up to Dailies with that week's exercise ready to screen, across all
twelve sessions, and take part in the review: not just presenting your
own work, but reading and responding to what the room brought.

## What to submit

Nothing separate. This mark is drawn from the Dailies sessions
themselves, as they happen — there is no artefact to hand in beyond
attending and contributing.

## How it's assessed

There is no per-artefact criterion, because there is no single artefact:
the teaching team records presence, whether the week's exercise was
actually ready to screen, and the quality of critique offered to other
students' work, then judges the semester as a whole rather than
averaging twelve separate scores.
