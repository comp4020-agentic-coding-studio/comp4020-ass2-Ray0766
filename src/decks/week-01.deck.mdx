# The Rig

_SLOP8760 · Week 1_

---

## What this studio teaches

Serialized vertical drama, produced almost entirely with generative video —
one shot, one hook, one episode, one season, in that order.

---

## The ramp

- Weeks 1–6: **anime** grammar, **single** shots
- Weeks 7–12: **live-action** grammar, **continuous** cuts
- Every technique is proven small before it's asked to carry a season

---

## Before a single frame: the budget question

VRAM, not price or processor speed, sets what you can run.

---

## Reading a spec sheet

- More VRAM → bigger models, higher resolution, longer clips
- Less VRAM → not slower. Often: **doesn't run at all**
- Know your number before you plan a shot around a model you've only read
  about

---

## Where the weights come from

Public model repositories: one base release, then a long tail of
community finetunes built on top of it.

---

## Licenses are not interchangeable

- Some: commercial use, no questions
- Some: research-only, non-commercial
- Some: a responsible-use clause on specific output, regardless of what you
  paid

Read the license before you build a shot around a checkpoint.

---

## The graph is the artefact

A node-based workflow engine: boxes are operations, wires carry data.

---

## Save the graph, not the frame

Today's frame isn't the point. The graph is a **recipe** — rerun it with a
new seed and get a comparable result.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## Render time is not shooting time

A five-second shot does not render in five seconds.

---

## Budget the wait

- Iteration compounds: three takes is three full renders, not one
- Run batches while you do something else
- Log timing now — Week 3 asks you to trust that log
