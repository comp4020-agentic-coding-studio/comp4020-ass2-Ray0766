Landscapes and machines generate clean; faces do not, and a slightly wrong
human reads as wrong to every viewer regardless of what else is right in the
shot. This week is entirely about the workarounds — reference-locked faces,
limited expression ranges, dialogue kept short — that keep a human performance
from breaking the illusion.
