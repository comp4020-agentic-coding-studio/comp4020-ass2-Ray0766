> Four sentences, said plainly, then said as a fight.

## What to do

Build the ladder from this week's exercise for one action: one line,
subject and action, the full four sentences, then the four sentences
with a beat and a negative prompt added. Then generate the
counter-example on purpose — the same four sentences put under demands
that contradict each other — so there's a deliberately bad take to read
alongside the good ones.

## What to submit

All five clips in ladder order with the counter-example marked, the
four sentences used for each, and a seed and parameter log for the set.
Alongside the clips, name which of the four sentences the counter-example
was actually fighting itself on.

## How it's assessed

Sentence grammar checks whether each tier's clip actually matches its
own subject, shot size, camera movement, and light, in that order of
priority. Counter-example diagnosis checks whether the named
contradiction is the real cause of the failure, not a guess dressed up
as an explanation. Log quality checks that the log reproduces any one
clip in the set without further explanation.
