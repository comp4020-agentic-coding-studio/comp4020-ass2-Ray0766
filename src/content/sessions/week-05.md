Bring your cold open with sound off and no introduction — the room starts
the clock the moment it starts playing, exactly as a scrolling viewer
would. Three seconds in, everyone privately writes stay or scroll before a
word is said aloud. Only after every vote is down does the maker reveal
which hook type they were aiming for, and the room checks the vote against
the aim. A hook that gets scrolled past doesn't get a second showing to
plead its case.
