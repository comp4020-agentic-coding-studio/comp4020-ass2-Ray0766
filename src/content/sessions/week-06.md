Bring all five clips in ladder order, their source keyframes, and your
one-line drift note for each. Every clip plays through once at speed
first, the way a viewer would see it, then again stepped through frame
by frame against its own keyframe — a clip that reads fine at speed can
still be losing the face or the prop the moment it's checked panel by
panel. Notes separate two questions per tier: did motion happen at all,
and did identity survive the motion that happened. A tier that adds a
motion prompt or a last frame and drifts worse than the tier before it is
a real finding — name which one, sketch, flat colour, or the added
control, actually caused it.

The room places each piece on this ladder: [replay the full recorded
run](/studio/#week-06:t1) before the session, tier by tier, log included.
