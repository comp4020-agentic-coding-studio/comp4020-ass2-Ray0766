Ten pieces of work, each due on a Friday. Seven small pieces close out the
generator and story-holding weeks at 3% each; the pilot itself carries 35%;
and week 12 closes the course with the showcase and Dailies participation.
Every piece is built from the exercises screened at Dailies in the weeks
before it, so nothing is asked for that has not already been rehearsed.
