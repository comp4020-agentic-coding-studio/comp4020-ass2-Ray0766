## What is real and what is not

Slop University is invented. So are its departments, its convener and its
tutor, the course code SLOP8760, and every piece of administrative furniture
around them — the room numbers, the consultation hours, the shared render
window in the policies. No such institution offers this course, and nobody
listed on the People page exists.

Everything about the craft is real. The techniques, the parameters and their
published ranges, the model names, the licence terms quoted on the slides and
the platform delivery specifications are all taken from primary sources, and
each week's reading links the page a claim came from. Where a source could not
be confirmed, the course says the technique generically rather than putting a
number on a slide.

The Studio is the same. Every clip and still on this site is a file the
convener's rig actually recorded, kept with the prompt, the seed and the
settings that produced it: all recorded on the convener's rig, and nothing is
generated in the browser. A student who reads this material and believes it
will hold correct beliefs about how the work is made — the fiction stops at
the institution.

## The rig

The recordings came off one machine, and the licences on what runs on it are
part of what this course teaches.

[Wan2.2](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2) is "licensed under the Apache
2.0 License". [MiniMax H3](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3) is
"released under the MiniMax H3 Community License Agreement", which is a
licence with conditions rather than a permissive one, and is worth reading
before a graduating student builds a business on it.
[NoobAI-XL](https://huggingface.co/Laxhar/noobai-XL-1.1) declares
`fair-ai-public-license-1.0-sd`, and its own model card adds a clause the
licence does not: "We prohibit any form of commercialization". Two documents,
read together, or the answer is wrong.

Reference stills for The Ninth Head were made with a commercial image
generator rather than on the local rig; they are inputs to the course's own
work, and no licence claim is made about them here.

## How this site is built

The site is an [Astro](https://astro.build/) 7 build. Its structure and
components come from `astro-course-university` and `astro-theme-university`,
its palette and lockups from `astro-theme-slop`, and the twelve decks are
rendered by [astromotion](https://github.com/ANUcybernetics/astromotion),
which builds a deck from the same Markdown the rest of the site is written in.
Type is set in Public Sans, with Roboto Mono for code and for anything that has
to line up in a column.

The course material on this site is published under
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). The
recordings are covered by the licences above, which are stricter, and win.
