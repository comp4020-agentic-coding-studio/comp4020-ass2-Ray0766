title: Vera Lin
description:
  Vera Lin spent eleven years writing for a long-running television soap
  opera, the last four as its head writer, before leaving broadcast for
  vertical drama the year the format found an audience larger than her old
  timeslot. She has never issued a public correction, a retraction, or an
  apology for a storyline, on the standing position that a season either
  works or it doesn't, and a statement afterward changes neither. She teaches
  the anime-to-live-action ramp the way she wrote for daytime television —
  one episode at a time, on a schedule that does not move for anyone's
  rewrite.
