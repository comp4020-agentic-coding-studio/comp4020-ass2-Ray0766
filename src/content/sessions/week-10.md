Bring the three finished shots and a note on what the quality gate
caught, or would have caught. From this week, Dailies runs at the speed
of the queue, not the speed of a crit: the batch screens back to back,
and each note is filed against the gate it belongs to — continuity,
loudness, or captions — rather than as open commentary on the shot.
Roles rotate through the room the way they rotated on the line: whoever
ran the continuity audit reports first, then edit and sound, then
whoever ran the gate itself. A shot that already failed the gate doesn't
get re-argued in the room. It goes back to the queue, patched or
reshot.
