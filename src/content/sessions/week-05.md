Bring all five clips in ladder order with the counter-example marked, not
just the one you'd have picked. Each clip screens once, cold, with no
framing from the maker beforehand — if a shot needs an introduction to
work, it isn't working yet. Notes are limited to the four sentence
elements from lecture: subject, shot size, camera movement, light. A note
that isn't phrased in one of those four terms — "I like it," "something's
off" — doesn't count and gets sent back before the room moves on. The
counter-example gets the same four-term treatment as the rest of the
ladder; the room names which of the four sentences it was actually
fighting itself on, not just that it looked strange.
