description:
  Dutch Meridian directed television commercials for just over thirty years,
  across every format from fifteen-second spots to the handful of
  feature-length campaigns nobody was allowed to cut down. Three decades
  convinced him that only the first three seconds of anything are actually
  being watched, and he built a career, then a teaching practice, entirely on
  that conviction. He retired from commercial work the year clients started
  asking for content instead of advertisements, on the grounds that the job
  hadn't changed, only the word for it. He watches student dailies with the
  same stopwatch discipline he once billed by.
