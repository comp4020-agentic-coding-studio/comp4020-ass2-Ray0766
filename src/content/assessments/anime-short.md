> A short scene told entirely in anime grammar.

## What to do

Produce one short scene, thirty to sixty seconds, told in anime grammar
throughout and framed for a vertical screen. The scene opens with a hook
built using one of the four types from Week 5, established inside the
first three seconds, and carries through to a cut edited and scored per
Week 6.

## What to submit

The finished vertical video, captions burned in; the character or setting
reference sheet built in Week 4 that every shot in the scene is checked
against; and a production log listing each shot's seed, parameters, and
workflow graph, in the order the shots appear in the final cut.

## How it's assessed

Hook is judged on whether the first three seconds alone, watched cold,
earn the rest of the scene. Craft covers anime-grammar execution — held
beats, expression work, consistency with the reference sheet — plus the
edit and sound choices from Week 6. Coherence checks that the scene reads
as one continuous piece of drama rather than a set of disconnected shots.
Production log is checked the way Week 3's logs are: another person should
be able to see how each shot was made without asking.
