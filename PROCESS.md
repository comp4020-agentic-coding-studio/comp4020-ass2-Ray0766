# Process overview

## What I built

SLOP8760, Slop Opera: a twelve-week studio course in serialized AI video drama as the site a student would use — twelve lectures with decks, twelve Dailies, ten assessed pieces summing to 100, a release calendar, three interactives, and a Studio that replays the recorded ladders. The machine shoots; only technique gets credit.

## How I got here

**What a good course looks like.** Assignment 1 feedback said the site was fine and the account of directing it was not; the harness came first ([518f35d](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Ray0766/commit/518f35d229a451a7bf275859561ca721ccdd0c03)): every line in it points at a failure that taught it. Before any check I wrote down six decisions — one idea carried through; what is assessed is what was taught (Biggs); a list of instruments (after *How to Make (Almost) Anything*); a ramp, not a tour; Dailies as the formative loop (Sadler); plain register (after CS 007) ([5fc4e14](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Ray0766/commit/5fc4e14734b4aa7da433196109bb1bddd32e4838)). Two calls beat the obvious ones: an instrument ladder over a topic syllabus, so every week adds one nameable machine the assessment then uses; anime before photoreal, because stylisation absorbs a generator's errors — the same slip reads as house style in week 4 and as failure in week 7.

**Which I encoded.** Five of the six are guarded by checks, each seen red first: the violation injected, the failure captured, reverted ([eaf6928](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Ray0766/commit/eaf6928ca68cdc4ee011372e12896e1b886455a8), [f229522](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Ray0766/commit/f22952287ff613cd6d53ea742af66003aa7f5a44), [d8d7c67](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Ray0766/commit/d8d7c67c6db2bba4b28f5e5592469e1b3c605ff0), [de5ce22](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Ray0766/commit/de5ce225d4a0a949b4aa0fc1ce4f02c0dd9741e8), [0488124](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Ray0766/commit/04881241ec3d3678b853af2bac6ed3993e523edb)). Two were red on real content immediately: five Dailies never said what to bring; three forward references named weeks that hadn't happened.

The pass that taught me most landed green, the agent reporting done ([e21b122](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Ray0766/commit/e21b1224609ae9766997161bb04981931f04d09b)). Read outside the agent, in a browser at both marking viewports and both themes, it had five faults no check could see: builder copy on student pages, my unlayered CSS restyling theme cards, a hero title unreadable on a phone, an unlooked-at light theme, a deck describing another course. The lesson was not the fixes: the agent's "done" and my checks measured the same thing, and neither was the page. I committed the broken pass, then directed the repair sentinel-first:

> Write spec/builder-text.test.ts first … confirm it is red for the right reason — it must name exactly the pages above.

Live generation stays out of a static site: the Studio replays my rig's recordings, one input at a time ([5c7365a](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Ray0766/commit/5c7365a9f9def8995fa86be717e7bd6ebca016c9), [9d314c4](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Ray0766/commit/9d314c46e4f367c7c8c970955f5d2c145d49aabb), [dc4e6f3](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Ray0766/commit/dc4e6f30245f94b1958fb1dbd0c0cbb487ec7c1e)):
