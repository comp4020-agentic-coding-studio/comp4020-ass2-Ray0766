Bring the first-draft series bible — character list, world rules, tone
page, and beat template. Bibles get read aloud, cover to cover, before the
room says anything — a document that only makes sense with its writer
narrating over it isn't finished yet. Questions go first to the episode
engine: could a stranger
write episode two from this alone, using nothing but the character list,
the world rules, and the beat template. Tone gets checked against the
reference footage already on file for the season, not just against the
page. A bible that only works read alongside its author goes back for
another pass, not forward to Week 11.
