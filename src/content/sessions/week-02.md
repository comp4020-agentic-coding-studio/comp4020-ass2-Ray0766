Bring the still you locked and all three takes, not just the one you'd have
picked. Each one-shot screens once, cold, with no framing from the maker
beforehand — if the shot needs an introduction to work, it isn't working
yet. Notes are limited to the four grammar elements from lecture: subject,
shot size, camera movement, light. A note that isn't phrased in one of
those four terms — "I like it," "something's off" — doesn't count and gets
sent back for a rewrite before the room moves on.
