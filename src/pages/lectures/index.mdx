Lectures run on Mondays, in four phases. Each page sets out the week's craft,
what to do before class, and the exercise that Wednesday's Dailies will
screen. Every week is delivered from slides, and each lecture opens its own;
the [twelve decks](/decks/) are also listed on one page, with the sources each
of them cites.
