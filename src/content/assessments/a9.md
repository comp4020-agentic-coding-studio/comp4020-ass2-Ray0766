> A beat is a state worth showing, not a feeling worth describing.

## What to do

Turn one premise into all four ladder tiers from this week's exercise
for the same two-to-three shot sequence: a prose paragraph, a beat sheet
written in states, a beat sheet with a sized shot list, and a boarded
version with keyframes tagged to their references. The sequence's
opening beat is its three-second hook, using one of the four hook
devices on purpose.

## What to submit

All four tiers for the same sequence, with the hook device named for the
opening beat and a reference or master plate tagged to every entry on
the shot list.

## How it's assessed

Beat sheet quality checks that every beat is written as a state rather
than a transition — something a shot could actually show, not a feeling
about how one moment turns into the next. Hook design checks whether the
named device actually matches what the opening beat does, not just that
one was named. Shot list completeness checks that every entry carries
both a size and a tagged reference, not composition alone.
