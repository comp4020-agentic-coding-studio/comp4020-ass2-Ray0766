> A keyframe is only as good as the log that can put it back.

## What to do

Climb the full five-tier prompt ladder from this week's exercise on one
subject: a single line, then subject, setting, and mood, then the
complete four-part grammar, then the tag vocabulary and negative prompt
added on top, then shot size, camera angle, and a colour script layered
over that. Keep the same subject through all five tiers, so what gets
judged is what the grammar adds, not a new idea invented at each step.

## What to submit

The top-tier keyframe with its full seed and parameter log, plus the
four tiers beneath it, labelled in ladder order.

## How it's assessed

Grammar precision checks whether the top tier actually uses all four
grammar elements plus a negative prompt, not just more words stacked on.
Range across tiers checks that each lower tier is a genuinely simpler
version of the same subject rather than a relabelled duplicate. Log
quality checks that the log alone, with nobody standing next to it,
reproduces the top-tier keyframe.
