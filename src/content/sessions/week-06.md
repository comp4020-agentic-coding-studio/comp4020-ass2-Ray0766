Bring your cut exported with captions burned in, on a phone if you have
one, because that's the screen it's actually watched on. The sequence
plays once through without pausing, the way a viewer would watch it,
before any shot-by-shot discussion starts. Notes are split by job: is a cut
landing on the right frame, is the music pushing or fighting the pace, is
there a silence before the turn or does the score run straight through it.
A cut nobody can hear properly doesn't get picture notes until the sound is
fixed.
