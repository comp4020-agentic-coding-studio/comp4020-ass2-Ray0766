> An audit is a list of items, not a feeling about a shot.

## What to do

Build all four reference-set tiers from this week's exercise, ending on
a full set with a scene master plate, then generate the same two-shot
scene against each tier and audit both shots item by item — face,
costume, key props, location — rather than judging the pair as a whole.

## What to submit

The four reference sets, the resulting two-shot scene for each tier,
and the audit notes: a pass or fail per item, per shot, per tier.

## How it's assessed

Audit rigor checks whether the notes are actually itemised — face,
costume, props, location — rather than one verdict standing in for all
of them. Reference completeness checks that the top tier's set actually
covers a full costume range and a scene master plate, not just a face.
Continuity result checks whether the top tier's audit shows fewer
failures than the lower tiers, which is the whole point of building the
set out that far.
