Bring your workflow graph and one rendered clip, not a screenshot — Dailies
runs from files that can be rerun, not from what you remember happening.
Each rig gets projected cold: if it doesn't run in front of the room, that's
the result, not a technical difficulty to explain away afterward. Notes go
on VRAM used, render time, and whether the graph is saved in a state someone
else could open and rerun. A rig that works but wasn't saved as a reusable
graph doesn't pass; save-as-you-go starts today.
